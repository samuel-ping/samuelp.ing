My roommate and I were walking through our neighborhood to pick up a weekday lunch. As we were walking, I noticed a large open plastic bag on the sidewalk partially filled with papers, presumably to be recycled. My eyes immediately locked on this thick binder resting on some other papers. Intrigued, I immediately walked over to take a closer look (prompting a confused sound from roomie, who didn't know why I stopped walking with him). I picked up the binder (inducing a groan), flipped it open to a random page, and to my delight, it was a movie script! After a quick skim I snapped the book shut and resumed my walk towards the restaurant, leaving my roommate dumbfounded in the dust. "Do NOT bring that in the apartment." "Don't worry it'll stay in my room!" And that was that.

On the way back to our apartment, I read some more of the script out loud. It was about how someone transformed into a turtle and someone else was trying to explain to the ex-human turtle's parents that she turned into a turtle. Anyways, Johnny Depp starred in this movie!
