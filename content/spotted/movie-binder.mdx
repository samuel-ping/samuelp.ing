My roommate and I were walking through our neighborhood to pick up a weekday lunch.

As we were walking, I noticed a large open plastic bag on the sidewalk partially filled with papers, presumably to be recycled. My eyes immediately locked on this thick binder resting on some other papers. Intrigued, I immediately walked over to take a closer look (prompting a confused sound from roomie, who was looking back at my wondering I wasn't walking with him). I reached into the bag (inducing a signature roomie groan) and picked up the binder (cue louder groan), flipped it open to a random page, and saw a familiar sight. To my delight, it was a script! After a quick skim, I snapped the book shut and resumed my walk towards the restaurant, leaving my roommate dumbfounded in the dust. "Do NOT bring that in the apartment." "Don't worry it'll stay in my room!"

On the way back to our apartment, I started reading the script out loud. It was about how someone transformed into a turtle and someone else was trying to explain to the ex-human turtle's parents that she turned into a turtle yadda yadda. Anyways, fun fact, Johnny Depp starred in this movie!
