It was a beautiful day in Central Park. The temperature was in the mid-70s, the sun was out, and I had just ran to the park to meet my roommate who took his sister's dog there for a walk, a task in his quest to give the mini pooch a perfect day.

After chilling in the grass for a bit, we started heading to the subway. As we were walking, an irregularity in the grass caught my eye. It blended in well, challenging me to claim it as my prize. And so, drawn to its verdancy, I picked it up off the ground.

When he saw me pick up yet another item from the NYC ground, he immediately groaned. "Don't you dare bring that filthy thing in the apartment!" And of course, bring it into the apartment I did.
