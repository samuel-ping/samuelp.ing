The item that started this all. I picked this up from the ground when walking home from dinner with my roommate. I stepped on it while crossing the street, then looked back and did a double take. A sealed daily-use contact lens?! Those things don't come cheap.

When I peered more closely at the label, it announced that the prescription was -8.50 power! Whoever dropped this must've had a very unsightly (ha) day.

When my roommate saw me pick up the item from the NYC ground, he immediately groaned. "Don't you dare bring that filthy thing in the apartment!" And bring it into the apartment I did. Who knows, maybe one of my friends has a -8.50 prescription and could use the lens! (Spoiler: none of my friends have stepped forward with a -8.50 power.)
