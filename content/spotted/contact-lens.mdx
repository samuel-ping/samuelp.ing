My first pickup from the lovely streets of New York! While walking home from dinner with my roommate. I stepped on something small and hard while crossing the street, slightly scraping the object across the ground. I looked down at the victim of my foot, and lo and behold, a sealed daily-use contact lens?! Those things don't come cheap.

When I peered more closely at the label, it told me that the prescription was -8.50 power! Whoever dropped this must've had a very unsightly (ha) day.

When my roommate saw me pick up the item from the ground, he immediately groaned. "Don't you dare bring that filthy thing in the apartment!" And bring it into the apartment I did. Who knows, maybe one of my friends has a -8.50 prescription and could use the lens! (Spoiler: none of my friends have stepped forward with -8.50 eyesight.)
