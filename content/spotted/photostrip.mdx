My friend and I were walking through LES when I noticed two photostrips lying on the ground at the entrance to a photobooth. I picked up one of the two identical strips. "Why would they leave these on the ground?" I asked. There are a whopping seven people in these photos, how could there be two left over? Did they somehow print nine strips by accident? Did this group of friends suddenly break up soon after the photos were taken and printed? Maybe they didn't actually all know each other but took photos together in a late night blur of shenanigans? So many questions. So little answers.

_By the way, if you are somehow one of the people in the strip and would like your photo removed, please let me know!_
