My friend and I were walking through LES when I noticed we were passing a step with two identical photo strips sitting on it. This was the entrance to a photobooth. I picked up one of the two strips to the amusement of my friend and showed her. "Why would they leave these on the ground?" I asked. There are a whopping seven people in these photos, how could there be two left over? Did they somehow print nine strips by accident? Did this group of friends suddenly break up soon after the photos were taken and printed? Maybe they didn't actually all know each other but took photos together in a late night swirl of shenanigans? So many questions. So little answers. I don't know any of the people in the strip, but I hold a piece of their fun night in my hands.

By the way, if you are one of the people in the strip and somehow stumbled across this small website and would like your photo removed, please let me know!
